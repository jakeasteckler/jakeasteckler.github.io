I’m a research scholar at GovAI, where I write about U.S. AI policy, national security, and geopolitics.

Previously, I was a legislative fellow in the U.S. Senate, a researcher at Harvard's Belfer Center, and an aviation officer in the U.S. Army, where I commanded AH‑64 Apache helicopters and RQ‑7B drones. I have also volunteered in Ukraine, where I conducted research and worked with organizations supporting the front lines.

I hold a B.S. from the United States Military Academy at West Point, an MPA from Harvard Kennedy School, and an MBA from MIT Sloan.
